Crop photos shown in the app (crop picker, scans list, calendar, results).

Put one photo per crop here, named after the crop key:

  cassava.jpg   beans.jpg   matooke.jpg   maize.jpg

- Square, at least 600 x 600 pixels, JPEG, under about 150 KB each.
- The crop should fill the frame (e.g. a cassava plant or roots, a handful of
  bean pods, a matooke bunch, a maize cob), in daylight.
- Only use photos you took yourself or that are licensed for reuse
  (e.g. CC0, CC BY). Record the photographer and licence in CREDITS.txt in
  this folder; CC BY photos must be credited in the app's About text.

A crop without a photo here keeps its drawn illustration, so photos can be
added one at a time.
