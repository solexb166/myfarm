Delete your MY FARM account

Kusazaamu akawunta yo eya MY FARM

You can delete your account and all your data at any time. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MY FARM and make sure you have internet.
  2. Tap Account at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Under Privacy, tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete for good to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email myfarm.afrotym@gmail.com with the subject Delete my MY FARM account, from the email address you sign in with, or include the phone number you sign in with.

To make sure the request comes from you, we may send a code to that phone number or email and ask you to reply with it. We delete the account within 7 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (phone number or email) and your name
  • All your scans and crop photos
  • Your season plan and tasks
  • Your location choice and home area

What is kept

  • If you signed in by phone, the record of sign-in SMS messages (your number and the time), used to stop misuse, is deleted after 7 days.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Area disease totals that were shown before you deleted your account (for example “12 farmers in Mbale”) are recalculated without your scans. They never contained your name or number.
