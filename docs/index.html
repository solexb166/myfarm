MY FARM

Your agronomist in your pocket

For farmers in Uganda. English and Luganda.

  • Find crop diseases from a photo of a leaf: cassava, beans, maize and more. Works without internet.
  • Get treatment advice: organic and chemical options, and how to prevent it next season. Listen to it read aloud.
  • Plan your season with a crop calendar of tasks.
  • See diseases near you: what other farmers in your district are finding.

Maize photo: “corn leaves” by normanack, Flickr, CC BY 2.0. Disease models trained on open datasets listed in MODELS.md.
