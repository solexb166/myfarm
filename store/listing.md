# Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store
listing. Play has no Luganda listing language, so the listing is in English;
the app itself is in English and Luganda.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
MY FARM: Crop Disease Doctor
```

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Find crop diseases from a leaf photo, offline. Advice in English and Luganda.
```

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
MY FARM is an agronomist in your pocket for farmers in Uganda. Take a photo of a sick leaf and find out what is wrong with your crop in seconds, even with no internet.

FIND CROP DISEASES FROM A PHOTO
• Cassava: mosaic disease, brown streak, bacterial blight, green mottle
• Beans: rust, angular leaf spot
• Matooke: black sigatoka, fusarium wilt
• Maize is coming soon
The disease check runs on your phone, so it works in the garden with no network and no data costs.

KNOW WHAT TO DO
For every disease you get clear advice: what causes it, organic treatment, chemical treatment and how to prevent it next season. Tap "Listen" to hear the advice read aloud.

SEE DISEASES NEAR YOU
Find out which diseases other farmers in your district are finding, and how to protect your crop before they reach your garden. Only totals are shown: names, phone numbers and photos are never shared.

PLAN YOUR SEASON
Get a crop calendar from planting to harvest, with weeding, fertiliser, spraying and scouting reminders. Tick off tasks as you go.

ENGLISH AND LUGANDA
Use the whole app in English or Luganda, and switch any time.

YOUR RECORDS ARE SAFE
Sign in once with your phone number or email. No password needed. Your scans and season plan are backed up to your account, so you keep them if you change phones. Location is only recorded if you agree, and only as your district.

MY FARM gives guidance from a photo, not a lab test. For serious or spreading problems, talk to your local agricultural extension officer.
```
